Real SFCW radar figures: real-hyperbola.png and real-data panel of bscan-comparison.png.
Mercedes Solla (2024), GPR dataset: pulsed radar and SFCW data for rebar detection (experimental data).
SFCW measurements supplied by Screening Eagle Technologies; Proceq GP8800, concrete specimen II.
Dataset: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.10962520
Associated article: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.prostr.2024.09.250
License of dataset: GPL-3.0-or-later. See GPL-3.0.txt and https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0.html

Changes for the figures: read SEG-Y/CSV, reconstruct time sampling from CSV, subtract trace DC offset and mean trace, normalize display by P99. No hyperbolas were drawn over the data. real-hyperbola.png retains the original 5 mm spatial sampling and shows a magnified selection. The comparison panel was smoothed and resampled to 5 cm x 50 ps and cropped to 1.4 m x [0,16) ns. Time zero and phase were not fitted to synthetic data. Source measurements are available at the dataset DOI.

All other figures depict project simulations or configuration geometry. Their values are scenario settings rather than measurements of a specific field site.
